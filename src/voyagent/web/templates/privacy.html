Privacy

What ReelTrip does with your data

Short version: no accounts, no ads, no tracking cookies.

What we store

  • Itinerary checks. The text you paste is used for the check and then discarded. The report (place names, what was found, the findings, and a name if you chose to put one on it) is stored under a random link so you can share it. If you ask for a fix, the rewritten itinerary is stored with its report.
  • Trip plans. The plan and the form answers behind it (destination, dates, interests, and where you travel from if you gave it) are stored under a random link.
  • Usage limits. To keep the free service available, we count requests per visitor per day. The count is kept against a scrambled (hashed) form of your IP address, not the address itself, and expires after two days.
  • Lookups. Place lookups, opening hours, driving times and search results are cached without any link to who asked.
  • Usage counts. We count events per day (visits, checks started, thumbs up or down). No cookies, no visitor ids, nothing about you.

Anyone who has a report or plan link can open it. The links are random and are not listed anywhere or offered to search engines, but treat a link like the document itself: share it only with people you want to see it. Don't paste names, booking references or other personal details into an itinerary.

What is sent to other services

  • AI models (Groq, and Google Gemini on its free tier) receive the itinerary text or trip request to read it or write a plan. Google states that free-tier Gemini requests may be used to improve its products.
  • OpenStreetMap (Nominatim, Overpass, map tiles) receives place names to look up, and your browser loads map images from it.
  • Web search (Serper, and Tavily when configured) receives search queries about places, such as a museum's opening hours.
  • Open-Meteo and AlAdhan receive a location and date for weather and prayer times.
  • OSRM (routing on OpenStreetMap data) receives the coordinates of a day's stops to measure driving time, and Nager.Date receives a country and year for public holidays.
  • Links marked "Google Maps" open Google Maps with a place or route; nothing is sent to Google unless you click one.
  • Hosting and storage: the site runs on Render and stores reports and plans in Upstash.
  • Your browser loads fonts from Google Fonts and scripts from cdnjs.

None of these services receive your name or email from us, because we never ask for them.

Removing something

To have a report or plan deleted, open a request here and include its link.

Limits of the check

ReelTrip looks places up on OpenStreetMap and reads published opening hours. It can miss real places, match the wrong one, or rely on hours that have changed. It is a second pair of eyes, not a guarantee: confirm anything your trip depends on.
