What gets checked

  • It exists. Each named place is looked up on OpenStreetMap. A place with no trace on the map or the web is flagged: AI planners do invent them.
  • It's where the plan says. A restaurant "in Kairouan" that is really 135 km away gets caught.
  • It's open that day. Museums and monuments are matched to their weekly closing day, with the source.
  • The day fits. Driving time between a day's stops is measured on real roads, and days with too much road or too many sights are called out.
  • Then it fixes it. One click rewrites the plan around the findings and checks the rewrite, so you see before and after.
  • Friday prayer and Ramadan, if you ask for them.

We say exactly how much was checked, never "everything". Your pasted text is not stored; only the report is.
